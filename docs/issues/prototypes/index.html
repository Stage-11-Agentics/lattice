Issue log: three directions

Filing is the same in all three. What differs is where issues live in the dashboard and what the screen is built to do. Each direction rests on a different belief about how the issue log will be used. All three run on the same 41 mock issues and 14 stories, and every action works.

Take A

Inbox

It assumesOne person clears a queue, and the job is to decide each issue fast.

Looks like
A queue on the left, the selected issue in full on the right.
Central move
One key per decision. The cursor moves on by itself.
Best when
Issues arrive in bursts after a QA sweep and need deciding in one sitting.
Weakest when
You only want to look something up.
Open Take A → Take B

Ledger

It assumesThe log is mostly read. The job is to see what happened to everything that was reported.

Looks like
One dense table. Every issue on a line, with its stories and their statuses.
Central move
Read across the row. Filter by state, filer or source. Switch to a by-story view.
Best when
The question is "what came of Sunday's sweep" or "what is LAT-377 meant to fix".
Weakest when
Forty new issues need deciding quickly.
Open Take B → Take C

On the board

It assumesNobody opens another tab. Issues belong beside the stories, on the board you already watch.

Looks like
The existing board, with a rail of loose issues at the left and markers on the cards.
Central move
Drag an issue onto a card to link it, or onto Backlog to make a story.
Best when
Issues are few and the board is where decisions get made.
Weakest when
The log grows past what a side rail can hold, or you need the closed ones.
